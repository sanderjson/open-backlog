# Move the project's ideas into the backlog

## Why
Ideas scattered over TODO comments, issues, notes and chat get lost; one list gets read.

## Acceptance
- Each open idea is an item here, with a status and a priority.
- The old lists point to the backlog instead of keeping their own copy.
