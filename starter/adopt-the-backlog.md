# Adopt the backlog

An epic: the items it lists under `items` are one piece of work, in the order to do them. Copy it
as a prompt from its sheet to hand all of it to an agent at once.

## Why
One list of work, next to the code, that people and agents both keep current.

## Acceptance
- Someone new to the project finds what to do next from the board alone.
- Every open idea about the project is an item, not a note somewhere else.
